## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of high-concept graphic editorial design and technical creative engineering. It captures the spirit of an avant-garde creative technologist: precise, daring, intellectually curious, and vibrantly alive.

### Personality & Tone
- **Architectural & Bold:** Decisive visual structures with monumental display typography and intentional asymmetry.
- **Hyper-Electric & Chromatic:** High-chroma saturated primaries against warm tactile substrates, generating deliberate tension.
- **Craft-Obsessed & Technical:** Technical specifications, benchmark readouts, and engineering metadata balanced against expressive creative art direction.
- **Kinetic & Tactile:** Micro-interactions built with spring physics, magnetic pull states, and crisp frame changes.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **High-Contrast Digital Editorial** and **Experimental Creative Technologist Brutalism**. The system rejects generic SaaS templates in favor of oversized typography, structured hairline dividers, stark field contrasts, and hyper-saturated accent gestures.

## Colors

The palette operates on dynamic surface alternation. Pages transition between warm gallery ivory substrates and deep obsidian technical containers. Electric accents cut through both environments to signal interaction, technical status, and narrative emphasis.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (Warm Ivory):** `#FAF8F5` provides an organic, tactile, paper-like background that keeps large text legible and softens high-density layouts.
- **Obsidian Dark Surface:** `#0A0E1A` drives stark contrast sections, terminal views, and deep footer panels.
- **Primary (Electric Cobalt):** `#1D4ED8` (base) and `#2563EB` (bright hover) anchor core calls to action, active navigation tabs, and primary interactive links.
- **Secondary (Vivid Electric Violet):** `#7C3AED` and `#8B5CF6` denote system architecture tags, experimental feature tags, and generative visual modules.
- **Tertiary (Radiant Coral):** `#EA580C` and `#F97316` indicate deployment state alerts, interactive focus accents, and case study hero moments.
- **Accent (Acid Lime):** `#84CC16` and `#A3E635` serve exclusively as high-voltage indicators: live deployment statuses, commit activity beacons, and interactive physics toggles.
- **Subtle Surface Tones:** Warm gray border `#E6E2DA`, neutral muted text `#525866`, dark neutral muted text `#94A3B8`.

### Contrast Rules
- Acid lime (`#84CC16` / `#A3E635`) must always be paired with dark surfaces (`#0A0E1A`) or dark text overlays for accessibility compliance.
- Vibrant cobalt and electric violet elements on ivory surfaces must maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast against text.
- Dark theme inversion zones utilize `#0A0E1A` canvas with `#FAF8F5` typography and high-voltage accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs dramatic display geometry with utilitarian engineering precision.

### Type Pairings & Roles
- **Display & Headlines (Syne):** Architectural, sculptural letterforms with heavy visual weight. Used for hero titles, section index headers, and case study titles. Must always carry tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to maintain editorial cohesion.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives (Space Grotesk):** Neo-grotesque structure with technical geometric nuances. Highly readable across long-form case studies, project synopses, and client testimonials.
- **Metadata & Data Overlays (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced, tabular numbers and uppercase descriptors for Git commits, tech stack chips, timestamps, live coordinates, and file metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts rely on an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid that breaks standard symmetrical conventions.

### Grid Rhythm & Composition
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2.5rem` gutters, `4rem` margins. Off-center column distributions (e.g., 5-col narrative paired with 7-col interactive preview).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` margins. Side-by-side elements collapse to staggered full-width blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins. Vertical editorial flow with horizontal scroll breakout sections for technical telemetry and project gallery strips.

### Spatial Logic
- Use macro spacing (`space-2xl`, `space-3xl`) between distinct thematic sections to let large typography breathe against massive color blocks.
- Dense data clusters (code blocks, stack matrices, telemetry) use tight internal spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to retain a compact terminal feel.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, blurred, skeuomorphic drop shadows. Depth is created through structural overlaps, hairline borders, hard offset shadows, and high-contrast color planes.

### Spatial Tiers
1. **Flat Canvas (Level 0):** Pure `#FAF8F5` or `#0A0E1A` background surfaces without border containment.
2. **Structural Panels (Level 1):** Bounded surfaces separated by a 1px solid border (`#E6E2DA` in light mode, `#1E2538` in dark mode).
3. **Hard Offset Elevation (Level 2 - Interactive):** Cards and active callout boxes cast a hard, zero-blur drop shadow: `4px 4px 0px #0A0E1A` on light surfaces or `4px 4px 0px #1D4ED8` on dark surfaces.
4. **Active Float / Press (Level 3):** Under hover or press, cards translate `-2px -2px` with shadow expanding to `6px 6px 0px`, or compress to `0px 0px 0px` on active engagement.
5. **Overlay HUD & Drawers (Level 4):** Monospace technical overlays feature a semi-translucent glass effect: `rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a 1px solid border in `#7C3AED` or `#84CC16`.

## Shapes

The system uses crisp, architectural geometry. Surfaces feel structured, machined, and precise.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard buttons, input elements, inline code snippets, and standard badges maintain tight, crisp corners.
- **Cards & Visual Containers (0.5rem / 8px):** Media containers, preview modules, and interactive work cards balance modern sharpness with slight corner relief.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Interactive status chips (e.g., "Available for Q3", "Live Demo") and category filters adopt full pill rounding (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata from structural content modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Electric Action:** Cobalt `#1D4ED8` background, `#FAF8F5` text, 4px corner radius, bold `Space Grotesk`. Solid 1px `#1D4ED8` border. On hover: shifts to `#2563EB` with a hard `3px 3px 0px #0A0E1A` shadow.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#0A0E1A` border (light mode) or `#FAF8F5` (dark mode). Inverts background and text color on hover via crisp cubic-bezier transitions.
- **Terminal / Code Action:** JetBrains Mono typography, dark `#0A0E1A` background, acid lime `#84CC16` text, subtle lime border. Accompanied by trailing bracket glyphs (`->` or `[]`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Tech Stack Badges:** Monospaced 11px uppercase (`JetBrains Mono`), rounded pill shape, 1px border. Rendered in subdued neutral tones with an accent border corresponding to the technology category (Cobalt for client, Violet for engine/graphics, Coral for backend).
- **Status Beacons:** Small pill container featuring an active pulsing green dot (`#84CC16`), uppercase tracking, conveying live production availability.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Card:** Split layout featuring high-saturation imagery, video canvas, or WebGL frame on one side, and structured narrative typography on the other.
- **Border Treatment:** 1px hairline border `#E6E2DA`. Cards sitting on obsidian sections use `#1E2538`.
- **Hover Behavior:** Image expands via subtle scale (`1.02`), hard offset shadow locks into place, title transitions to electric cobalt or violet.

### Input Fields
- Monospace labels placed directly above the field with technical line indices (`01 // NAME`, `02 // EMAIL`).
- Crisp 1px `#0A0E1A` border on warm ivory substrate.
- Focus state triggers a 2px electric cobalt `#1D4ED8` border and a hard offset glow without default system outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 2px rounded square for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive state: 1.5px solid `#0A0E1A` border with transparent interior.
- Selected state: Filled solid with Electric Violet `#7C3AED` featuring a white checkmark or center pip.

### Interactive Telemetry & Code Readouts
- Terminal-inspired data boxes embedded into case studies.
- Display frame rate, bundle size, WebGL draw calls, and git hashes using `JetBrains Mono` at `11px` over `#0A0E1A` with Acid Lime `#A3E635` accents.
